Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Fortune Cookie Wisdom for Developers"**
(`u-gentle-koala-60-fortune-cookie-wisdom-for-developers-025ccf44`).

`main.py` writes exactly one original fortune-cookie line for a software
developer to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Deploy on Friday, and the weekend will teach you what your tests were too polite to say.

One short balanced sentence (88 characters): a true Friday-deploy fear,
turned on the quiet idea that your tests knew all along — they were just too
polite to say so.
